ACME CORP - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

This is a sample document for trying DocuBot out. Index it, then ask questions
like "how many vacation days do I get?" or "can I work remotely?".

## 1. Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, accruing monthly
from the start date. Unused days may be carried over into the next calendar
year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Days beyond that are forfeited on 31 December.

Part-time employees accrue vacation pro rata based on contracted hours.

## 2. Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from a direct manager and are reviewed every 6 months.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone.

## 3. Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop and a one-time 500 USD home office allowance.
Equipment remains company property and must be returned when leaving.

## 4. Expenses

Expenses under 100 USD do not need pre-approval but require a receipt. Anything
above 100 USD needs manager approval before the spend. Reimbursement is paid
with the next payroll run after submission.
